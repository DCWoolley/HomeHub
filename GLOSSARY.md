# HomeHub

HomeHub includes planning for household consumables and purchases.

## Language

**Household**:
A group of people sharing a Household Items Catalogue and Expected Weekly Usage amounts.

**Household Manager**:
The Household member responsible for managing membership. The creator is the initial manager and can transfer that responsibility to another member.
_Avoid_: Household Creator (when referring to the current membership-management role)

**Household Invitation**:
An offer to join a Household that grants membership only when accepted.

**Archived Household Item**:
A Household Item removed from active planning but retained so it can be restored.

**Planning Period**:
A positive whole number of weeks over which Expected Weekly Usage is totalled.

**Household Item**:
A non-food household consumable, such as toilet roll, washing-up liquid, bin bags, shampoo, or laundry detergent.
_Avoid_: Grocery item, ingredient

**Household Items Catalogue**:
The reusable list of Household Items available for planning within a Household. Each item has a default unit for expressing its usage.
_Avoid_: Groceries items list

**Expected Weekly Usage**:
The recurring quantity of a Household Item expected to be consumed in a week, used to help plan purchases. It may be fractional and remains the baseline until changed.
_Avoid_: Actual usage, purchase quantity
